Studio of Architecture and Design

Ekhaya Architects is a design-led architecture and interiors studio creating thoughtful, nature-responsive spaces. The studio specialises in homes, retreats, and bespoke projects that balance functionality, aesthetics, and context.

Every design is shaped through close collaboration, detailed planning, and a deep respect for natural surroundings resulting in spaces that are personal, purposeful, and timeless. At Ekhaya, we don't just design spaces; we create environments meant to be lived, felt, and remembered.
