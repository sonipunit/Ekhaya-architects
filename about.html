ABOUT THE COMPANY

At Ekhaya, we believe a space should feel like it belongs to its place, to its people, and to the life that unfolds within it.

From homes nestled in the landscapes of Coorg to quiet urban residences, retreats, cafés across Mysore and Bangalore to spaces shaped around wellbeing, our work is guided by one idea: design should add to the way we live, not compete with it.

We are drawn to the quiet things natural light, honest materials, familiar textures, considered proportions, and the relationship between a space and its surroundings.

We believe in restraint over excess, character over trends, and creating spaces that become more meaningful with time.

Ekhaya means home. And for us, home is not a style or a particular aesthetic. It is a feeling of belonging something we hope every space we create can hold, wherever it may be.
